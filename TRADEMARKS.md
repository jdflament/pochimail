The Pochimail **code** is licensed under [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE). The **brand** is separate and is not covered by that license:

- the names **Pochimail** and **Pochi**,
- the logo, wordmark and app icon,
- the mascot artwork (the plush envelope character, in every pose and colorway).

Brand and mascot assets are creative works of the project and are licensed under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 (see `packages/ui/src/assets/brand/LICENSE`, `packages/ui/src/assets/mascot/LICENSE` and `apps/desktop/build/LICENSE`).

## What you can do
- Use, study, modify and fork the code under the AGPL-3.0.
- Refer to the project by name to describe it accurately ("a fork of Pochimail").
- Build the app locally for your own use.

## What you must do if you redistribute
Redistributed or modified builds (forks, repackaged apps, hosted versions) **must use a different name and a different icon and mascot**, and must not suggest they are the official project.

## Official builds
Official builds are the signed releases published on GitHub Releases and linked from getpochimail.com. Only official builds use the project's verified Google and Microsoft OAuth client IDs. Forks and development builds register their own (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).

Questions about brand use: open a GitHub discussion or issue.
